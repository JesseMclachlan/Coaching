Intensity Meter
What is intensity? In calisthenics, intensity reflects how close you're working to your limit — measured by proximity to form breakdown, not just load. The zones below define effort bands and when each is appropriate.
0–30%
Warm-Up
Just getting blood moving. No real effort. Joint prep, activation, and mobility work.
30–50%
In Reserve
A solid amount in reserve. Only the slightest hint of effort on the last rep.
50–70%
Real Strain
You feel real strain and holding good form becomes difficult — but form does not break down.
70%+
At The Limit
Form breaks down. You are pushing the limit and forcing adaptation. Used mainly on intense days to break through plateaus.
Submax / Volume Day → 30–70% intensity
ME / Intensity Day → peaks at 70%+
